Open your investment file

Pick your Excel file from the Files app. The app reads your deposits and value right here on this device and works out your returns.

  • Nothing leaves this device. There’s no server or account, and the page is locked so it can’t send data over the internet. Nothing is uploaded. Your file is read inside this page, which can’t reach other websites and doesn’t store anything in your Claude account.
  • Your Excel file stays the only copy. Tap Save to write your changes back to it.
  • Works offline once it’s on your Home Screen.

          File & privacy

          Your file

          On iPhone, Save opens the share sheet. Choose Save to Files, pick the folder your file is in, and tap Replace. Keep the file in On My iPhone if you don’t want it synced to iCloud.

          Privacy

          • No account, no server, no analytics, and no code from other websites.
          • A security policy built into the page blocks every network request, so it can’t send your numbers anywhere.
          • Inside the Claude app this page can’t reach other websites, and it stores nothing in your Claude account.
          • Your numbers are read from your Excel file and written back only when you tap Save.
